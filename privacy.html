Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ebb is built so that your health information never leaves your phone unless you decide to share it.

What we collect

Nothing. Ebb has no account, no server and no analytics or advertising SDKs. The doses, weights, side effects, check-ins and settings you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Backups and transfers

If Android backup is turned on, your Ebb data is included in your personal Google backup and device-to-device transfer, encrypted by Android. You can also create a backup file or a CSV export yourself; those files go only where you save or send them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. Ebb only receives a confirmation that a purchase is active; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Children

Ebb is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Your choices

You can export or permanently erase all of your data from Settings at any time. Uninstalling the app also deletes it from the device.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the app's Google Play page.
